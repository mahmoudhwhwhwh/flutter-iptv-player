# 2027 Source Audit — 2026-08-26

The live custom menu endpoint is `https://iptv-subscription-api.tvkora56.workers.dev/v1/custom/menu` and returned HTTP 200 with a JSON list of 32 entries.

Entries 0–5 use Worker URLs under `https://iptv-subscription-api.tvkora56.workers.dev/v1/custom/stream/{index}.ts?code=2027`. Direct checks for indices 0–5 returned HTTP 200, content type `video/mp2t`, and substantial MPEG-TS byte samples. These are single progressive TS streams from the app's perspective and therefore do not expose adaptive quality tracks by themselves.

Entries 6–11 use the direct HLS manifests `https://live-football-2mf.pages.dev/index_bein%20max1.m3u8` through `index_bein%20max6.m3u8`. The tested manifests returned HTTP 200 and `application/vnd.apple.mpegurl`. Manifest 1 and 4 each declared three variants: 1920x1080 at 2.5 Mbps, 1280x720 at 5 Mbps, and 854x480 at 1.2 Mbps.

The HLS variants point to HTTP URLs on `185.191.126.127:8080` and include credentials/path segments. Direct variant probing from the sandbox was inconsistent: some attempts returned status 000 and one returned 462/empty content type. This indicates that the quality variants are not reliably reachable from the test environment and may be blocked by cleartext policy, upstream authorization, or source availability. The app cannot create working HD/SD variants if the manifest's child URLs are invalid or inaccessible.

The Flutter classifier correctly recognizes Worker custom `.ts` URLs as progressive transport streams and avoids WebView. The remaining 2027 issue is therefore split: the first six have no adaptive manifest at the app layer, while the later HLS channels need a valid authenticated proxy/rewrite for their child URLs or corrected upstream sources before internal quality switching can work reliably.
